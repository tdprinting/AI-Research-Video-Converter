Video Shrinker

Turns long iPhone videos into small MP4 files you can upload to Claude for research. Everything runs on your device; the video is never uploaded anywhere.

Settings

Keep this tab open and your screen on until it finishes.

Your files

    How it keeps quality high
    • It works out the exact bitrate your size limit allows, then picks the resolution and frame rate that give each frame the most detail for that budget.
    • “Sharpest frames” lowers the frame rate before the resolution. AI models look at individual frames sampled from a video, so clear frames matter more than smooth motion.
    • Resolution is capped at 1080p, since models shrink larger frames anyway.
    • Speech audio is encoded as small mono AAC so more of the file goes to the picture.
    • If the encoder overshoots the limit, the file is re-encoded at a corrected bitrate so it always fits.
    • For very long videos, splitting into several files keeps each one sharp.
